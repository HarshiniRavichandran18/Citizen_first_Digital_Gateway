E05
Income Certificate applications can require several documents, including address proof, land/property details, family/smart card, salary certificate and PAN card - TNeGA FAQ

E06
Citizens can check application status online using their application number - TNeGA FAQ

E09
Tamil Nadu describes e-Sevai centres as front-end delivery points for government and other services, particularly for rural citizens - Tamil Nadu IT Department

E10
